        💡How it works

        This shows each group's official, public schedule. It does not track anyone in real time: the chibi stands on the next confirmed date, not where they are right now.

        Times are approximate (8 pm local time when the source has none) and so are venue coordinates. Dates marked as announced come from a single announcement and may change.

        ✨What's new
          🔒Privacy

          The radar has no accounts, no tracking cookies and no analytics. Your browser (localStorage) keeps your city, your language, whether you muted sounds, your favorite groups and your bias, which alerts you already saw and what you picked in "Get alerts" (groups, types and country). None of it leaves your device.

          When you open it, the radar asks your browser for your location. If you allow it, the position is rounded (about 1 km) and stored only on your device to measure distances; if not, we use Bucaramanga or the city you choose. Your location never leaves your device.

          Fonts load from Google Fonts. Release covers come from Apple Music and YouTube, and the city map (only if you open it) from OpenFreeMap. Each of those services sees your IP address, like on any site that uses them. To erase everything, use "Delete my data" or clear this site's data in your browser.

          Data controller: Cristian Pérez (cristianperez.me). Alerts arrive through ntfy (ntfy.sh) or Discord: the radar posts to public topics and channels and you choose which to subscribe to from your app, so the radar never knows your number, email or location.
