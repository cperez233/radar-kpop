## 4.0 · 2026-10-15 · More alive: new chibis, city map and a top menu

- Chibis redrawn in K-pop sticker style: glossy hair locks, big gradient eyes, they blink on their own, breathe and hop when tapped. Every current member of all 8 groups.
- Fixed why phones showed old versions: the service worker now fetches the page, code and data from the network first, and every build is versioned.
- Globe: vertical drags no longer move the page, it stays where you leave it (no bounce, no vertical inertia), it moves while a group is selected, and auto-rotate stops when you touch it (with a button to turn it back on).
- Location: requested with a tap so the browser always shows its prompt, and the screen says whether it is real, picked or default, with the reason and how to enable it on iPhone, Android or desktop.
- Top menu with an animated pill that follows the section you are in; the bottom dock is gone.
- Numbered sections, each with its own colour, tape and entrance animation.
- City map for each stop (MapLibre + OpenFreeMap, free and keyless) in pastel colours, with 3D buildings and the group as chibis on the venue.
- New Releases and videos section: the latest music (iTunes Search API) and official YouTube uploads, no keys needed.
- Video mode rebuilt: title, globe and card in separate zones that never overlap, and only the featured group on the globe.
- The whole page in English in EN mode (alerts, notices, cities, tours, sources and accessibility labels), with an animation when switching language.
- News: allkpop blocks bots, replaced with Koreaboo.
- ENHYPEN shows a note about Heeseung, its seventh member, who has gone on as a soloist since March 2026. If a release cover fails to load, the group chibi shows instead.
- Privacy: a contact email to see, fix or delete your data.
