Generated by `build.py` from `changelog.json`. The site shows the same list (in Spanish or English) under "Novedades".

## 3.0 · 2026-10-15 · Member chibis, sightings and a zoomable map

- 55 new chibis, one per member, SD style: big heads, huge eyes, they breathe on their own and hop when you tap them.
- Three more groups: SEVENTEEN (JxJ unit tour), ENHYPEN (Blood Saga) and NewJeans.
- Official photos of all eight groups on the cards, shown whole, never cropped.
- Mobile map: rotating no longer scrolls the page, pinch to zoom, plus zoom in, zoom out and center-on-me buttons.
- Your real location is requested on load; if you deny it or it fails, we use Bucaramanga and tell you.
- Sightings near you: official X posts that mention a city, sorted by distance to you, with a news ticker and a heads-up when new ones arrive.
- Sounds and animations when you tap chibis, open panels or a sighting shows up.
- Mini trivia per group and a ticket on-sale sticker when a show has a sale date.
- Phone tab for push notifications, plus a server ready for Cloudflare Workers (backend/).
- Group notes in English too. Fixed the Stray Kids YouTube channel and the LNGSHOT X account.
